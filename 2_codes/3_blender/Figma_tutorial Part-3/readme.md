Press Ctrl + D to duplicate a frame. To move around the canvas, we can use the Hand Tool (H). For zooming in and out, hold the Ctrl key and scroll the mouse wheel.  
  
We can also adjust the zoom from the top-right corner and choose different zoom options. There are also shortcut keys available. For example, press Shift + 1 to Zoom to Fit, which fits all the content on the screen. Shift + 1 focuses on the overall canvas, while Shift + 2 focuses on the selected object or frame.  
  
For example, on the left-hand side, I have three different frames. To rename a layer, double-click its name and enter the new name.  
  
To keep the frames organized separately, I click on Page 1 and rename it Phone. Then, by clicking the + icon, I can add another page and name it Tablet. When I click on the Phone page, I can see all the frames associated with it.  
  
We can also move different frames between pages. To do this, right-click the frame, select Move to Page, and then choose Tablet.  
  
To focus on a frame inside the canvas, select it and press Shift + 2, which zooms directly into the selection.  
  
To add a background to the home screen, go to the Design panel on the right-hand side and click Fill. From there, we can select different background options such as HEX, RGB, CSS, HSL, HSB, and Gradient. We can also use an image or video as the background.  
  
For example, click Image, then select Choose Image and double-click the desired image. The image will be applied to the background. We can crop the image as needed. By holding the Shift key while dragging, we can maintain the image's proportions and aspect ratio.  
  
Under Stroke, click the + icon and drag with the mouse to increase the stroke width. The stroke can be adjusted from the Top, Bottom, Left, Right, Center, Inside, or Outside positions. To remove the stroke, simply click the – icon.  
  
Under Effects, click the + icon to add effects such as Inner Shadow, Drop Shadow, Layer Blur, and Background Blur. When an effect is no longer needed, click the remove/delete option.  
  
At the top toolbar, click the Rectangle (R) tool to access different shape and drawing options, including Rectangle, Line, Arrow, Ellipse, Polygon, Star, Place Image, and Place Video.  
  
For example, I select the Rectangle tool, draw a rectangle, and move it into another frame named Home Screen. When I place it inside the Home Screen frame, it becomes part of that frame's layers.
